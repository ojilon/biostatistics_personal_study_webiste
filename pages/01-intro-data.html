Core Theory

What is Biological Data?

In biostatistics, data consists of values or attributes recorded from biological entities—such as crop yields, animal body mass, pathogen incidence, or physiological rates. Accurate statistical analysis depends entirely on identifying the correct data type before choosing statistical tests.

📌 Key Takeaway

The choice of descriptive statistics (mean vs. median) and hypothesis tests ($t$-test vs. Chi-square) is determined by whether your variable is qualitative or quantitative.

Measurement Framework

The Four Levels of Measurement

1

Nominal Scale

Assigns labels or names to observations. Operations permitted: Counting ($N$) and calculating proportions or mode.

Example: Cattle breed (Ankole Longhorn, Friesian, Zebu).

2

Ordinal Scale

Ranks data in order, but the intervals between ranks are unequal or unknown. Operations: Median, percentiles.

Example: Soil fertility rank (Low, Medium, High).

3

Interval Scale

Ordered scale with equal intervals between units, but no true zero point (zero is arbitrary).

Example: Soil temperature in Celsius (°C).

4

Ratio Scale

Ordered scale with equal intervals AND a true absolute zero point (meaning absence of quantity).

Example: Bean root dry weight (grams) or canopy height (cm).

🌱 Field Practical Exercise

A researcher at the National Agricultural Research Organisation (NARO) collected the following parameters from a bean trial in Namulonge:

  • Var A: Leaf area ($cm^2$)
  • Var B: Resistance status (Resistant, Moderately Susceptible, Susceptible)
  • Var C: Pod count per plant
  • Var D: Flower color (White, Purple, Pink)

Identify the scale of measurement for each variable before performing analysis in SPSS or Excel.

💡 Knowledge Check

Question: Why can you calculate the arithmetic mean for leaf area (Ratio scale), but NOT for disease severity grade (Ordinal scale)?

Answer: On an ordinal scale (e.g., Disease Grade 1 to 5), the distance between Grade 1 and Grade 2 is not necessarily equal to the distance between Grade 4 and Grade 5. Because numeric intervals are not uniform, mathematical operations like addition and division are invalid. Ordinal data must be summarized using the Median or Mode rather than the Mean.
