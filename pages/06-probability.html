Core Theory

Probability in Biological Systems

Probability ($P$) quantifies likelihood between $0$ and $1$. Distributions underpin inferential statistics.

📌 Rules

$$P(A \text{ or } B) = P(A) + P(B)$$ (mutually exclusive)

$$P(A \text{ and } B) = P(A) \cdot P(B)$$ (independent)

🥚 Egg Weight Probability

$\mu = 60.0$ g, $\sigma = 2.5$ g. $P(X > 65)$:

$$Z = \frac{65-60}{2.5} = 2.00 \rightarrow P = 0.0228\ (2.28\%)$$

💡 Knowledge Check

Question: Ticks occur at $\lambda = 2.0$ per animal. Which distribution for $P(0$ ticks$)$?

Answer: Poisson. $P(X=0) = e^{-2.0} \approx 0.1353$ (13.53%).
