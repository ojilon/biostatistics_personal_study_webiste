Core Theory

Modeling Biological Relationships

Correlation evaluates linear association strength. Simple Linear Regression models $Y$ from $X$ for prediction.

📌 Equations

$$r = \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sqrt{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2 \sum (y_i - \bar{y})^2}}$$

$$\hat{Y} = \beta_0 + \beta_1 X$$

🌽 Field Practical: Fertilizer vs. Maize Yield

$$\hat{Y} = 2.15 + 0.038 X$$ · $r = 0.89$ · $R^2 = 0.792$ (79.2%)

Each extra 1 kg/ha nitrogen increases yield by 0.038 t/ha. Nitrogen explains 79.2% of yield variance.

💡 Knowledge Check

Question: $r = -0.84$ between tick count and daily weight gain. What does this mean? Does it prove ticks cause weight loss?

Answer: Strong inverse linear relationship. Correlation does not imply causation — confounders (nutrition, disease) may drive both.
